# Eight Seedance prompts with original video examples

Each prompt below accompanies an original [SuperSeedance example](https://superseedance.si/en/blog/seedance-prompt-guide#motion-examples). Source-generation settings: Seedance 2.5, text-to-video, 6 seconds, 720p, 16:9, no audio, non-draft. A repeated generation can differ from the example.

## 1. Rainy Night Parkour

[Watch the example and review its settings](https://superseedance.si/en/blog/seedance-prompt-guide#motion-01-rain-vault)

```text
A continuous six-second live-action film shot. An empty outdoor station walkway on a rainy night, with teal-blue ambient light alternating with warm yellow lamps. An adult runner in a dark red waterproof jacket is already sprinting at full speed from the left of the frame. The runner plants one hand on a waist-high railing, vaults over it, lands on both feet in a broad splash, and keeps running out to the right. The camera tracks parallel at a low angle. Columns rush past in the foreground, and the runner visibly travels through the frame. Show the complete causal sequence: hand contact, takeoff, descent, landing, and continued running. Normal action speed; the jacket hem and rainwater carry momentum. Realistic skin and reflections on wet ground, a clear full-body silhouette, and a coherent route through the setting. An original character, with no subtitles or branded graphics.
```

## 2. Castle in the Clouds

[Watch the example and review its settings](https://superseedance.si/en/blog/seedance-prompt-guide#motion-02-cloud-city)

```text
A six-second first-person FPV flight through a stone city high above the clouds, with realistic cinematic detail. Begin already flying fast along a narrow terrace. Bank into a passage curving to the right and fly through the open center of a huge round arch ahead. Immediately beyond it, a sunlit floating city and a rolling sea of clouds open into view. One continuous flight: the arch rapidly grows larger as the camera approaches, and its edges sweep past all sides of the frame. Once through, the distant cloud city is fully revealed, with clear differences in the speeds of the foreground, middle ground, and background. Keep the flight corridor readable, and have the camera physically pass through the opening. A vast yet detailed vista, with convincing stone, mist, and backlighting. No on-screen interface, wings, subtitles, or branded graphics.
```

## 3. Anime Roller Skating

[Watch the example and review its settings](https://superseedance.si/en/blog/seedance-prompt-guide#motion-03-anime-drift)

```text
Six seconds of original 2D cel-animated action on an elevated stone bridge under an orange sunset. Bold linework with varied thickness, teal-blue shadows, and a hand-painted background. An adult roller-skating courier wearing round goggles and a short blue cape is already approaching fast. The courier lowers their center of gravity, leans through a bend in the bridge, then slides both skates sideways to brake to a stop in the foreground. The camera tracks parallel from the side as the bridge railing rushes past. The lean of the body, extension of the legs, and snap of the cape make the changes in speed clearly visible. The direction of the turn must agree with the wheels’ contact with the ground. Allow deliberate hand-drawn smears, squash and stretch, and brushstroke speed lines, while keeping the same character and cel-animation style. One clearly readable skating turn throughout, with no subtitles or branded graphics.
```

## 4. Ink-Wash Whale

[Watch the example and review its settings](https://superseedance.si/en/blog/seedance-prompt-guide#motion-04-ink-breach)

```text
Six seconds of dynamic ink-wash animation, with visible xuan-paper fibers, black-and-white ink, and one small, unoccupied vermilion boat. The white space of a vast paper sea shifts with the waves. Begin as a giant ink whale is breaking the surface. In one powerful leap, its body traces a broad arc and rolls to reveal its belly. Its tail sweeps out scattered ink droplets before the whale falls back into the sea, sending weighty ink waves surging outward. The small vermilion boat glides forward with the swell. A fixed, distant side-view wide shot contains the entire leap. The whale crosses a substantial distance and height within the frame; do not substitute zooming for its movement. Dry-brush and wet-ink marks redraw naturally with the bending body. Give the waves clear direction and weight, and preserve the ink-wash material throughout. No subtitles or branded graphics.
```

## 5. Solo Dance

[Watch the example and review its settings](https://superseedance.si/en/blog/seedance-prompt-guide#motion-05-silk-dance)

```text
A six-second refined dance film on a dark, empty stage, with soft overhead light and a narrow shaft of side light. An adult dancer in a scarlet costume with long sleeves is stepping into a windup, completes one powerful full-body turn, and lets the sleeves fan outward into broad silk arcs under centrifugal force. The dancer flows into a low lunge to finish, arms open while the silk continues to fall. Keep the full body in view as the camera travels around the outside of the dancer through roughly a quarter-circle. The body’s orientation and camera angle change together. Show clear foot contact, weight transfer through the knees and hips, and the fabric’s inertia. Normal speed, realistic skin, silk fibers, and stage lighting. Do not hide the movement with slow motion or rapid cuts. An original character, with no subtitles or branded graphics.
```

## 6. Clay Animation

[Watch the example and review its settings](https://superseedance.si/en/blog/seedance-prompt-guide#motion-06-clay-bounce)

```text
Six seconds of handmade clay stop-motion animation on a miniature cobalt-blue and turmeric-yellow stage. Preserve fingerprints and sculpting marks in the clay. An original small orange clay creature drops diagonally from the upper left toward a round drum. Both feet depress the drumhead as its body visibly squashes. The drumhead springs back and launches it high into the air. Still carrying its rightward momentum, the creature stretches out its limbs in midair and follows an arc onto a soft cushion to the right of the drum, making a deep dent on landing. A fixed low-angle wide shot contains the drum, cushion, and full height of the jump. Exaggerated squash and stretch in the creature and drumhead drives the action, with distinct pauses and bursts of energy. Steady miniature side lighting; keep the creature’s color and facial features recognizable. Allow frame-by-frame stop-motion movement without turning the scene into smooth CGI. No subtitles or branded graphics.
```

## 7. Forest Adventure

[Watch the example and review its settings](https://superseedance.si/en/blog/seedance-prompt-guide#motion-07-forest-swing)

```text
Six seconds of stylized 3D adventure animation, with sunlight passing through giant translucent green leaves. An original small scarlet creature wearing a short scarf already holds a vine hanging from a high anchor point with both paws. It swings down from the left along a broad arc, speeding through a curtain of dew beneath hanging leaf tips at the lowest point. Its momentum carries it up toward a lower moss-covered platform on the right. As it approaches the platform, it releases the vine, lands on both feet, and bends its knees to absorb the impact. An oblique side-view wide shot establishes the vine’s anchor, the swing arc, and the landing point, containing the full path from descent to ascent. The vine pulls taut under the load, the tail counterbalances, the scarf trails behind, and droplets scatter in the creature’s wake. Detailed fur, leaves, and transmitted light. No subtitles or branded graphics.
```

## 8. Liquid Metal

[Watch the example and review its settings](https://superseedance.si/en/blog/seedance-prompt-guide#motion-08-liquid-chrome)

```text
A six-second abstract material art film. In a black space, a tightly coiled ribbon of liquid chrome reflects cobalt blue, silver-white, and amber light. The coil begins releasing from its center immediately. In one clear elastic unfurling, the heavy metal ribbon flings outward to form an open three-dimensional spiral extending through the foreground and background. Droplets fly from its edges, and the spiral retains a slight residual oscillation at the end. A fixed close shot contains the full range of deformation as the subject changes from a compact silhouette into a broad structure. Show motion through genuine changes in contour, occlusion, and curved surfaces, rather than only changing colors or reflections. Keep the metal ribbon continuous and the liquid thickness convincing, with clear tonal separation between sharp highlights and deep blacks. No white flashes, subtitles, or branded graphics.
```
